How it works

  1. Order and pay by the cut-off. You pay the full price now; the difference comes back after the lock. Try sizes at the Saturday rides of 28 Nov and 5 Dec.
  2. Final price set at the lock. On Wed 16 Dec 2026 each item takes the step its total reached; the difference comes back to you by hand.
  3. Collect at a PPK Saturday ride, Spedahub or Hubert's place (Green Lane). No post.

The small print

  • An item is made only if enough are ordered and paid by the cut-off (each item shows how many it needs); if not, you get a full refund for it.
  • Cancel for a full refund until the lock, Wed 16 Dec 2026. After that, no refunds except for defects; size swaps only from spares.
  • Kit not collected within 8 weeks of arrival gets one last message, then goes to club stock with no refund.
  • We use your name, contact and sizes only for this order, and share only sizes and printed names with the maker.
