Getting there

Each car is a listing on Group rides, titled "Taiping trip · Car 1 · …". Book a seat with your PPK account. Pickup points are public places, never a member's home.

Share tolls and petrol with your driver.Cars are arranged between members. PPK's ride terms cover the cycling, not the drive.

Race day

Bring

  • Helmet, and front and rear lights
  • Your bib and ID
  • Spare tube, pump and tools
  • Water and food for your distance
  • PPK kit for the group photo

The organiser's rules

Your rider bib and bike flag number are compulsory at check-in. On the 90 and 65 km, fix the bike bib with the timing chip to the front of the bike, 1 cm clear of any carbon. Wear a helmet and gloves. The 90 and 65 km routes split at the 4.8 km junction: follow the route signs and the marshals.

Checked 17 Oct 2026 against facebook.com (1, 2).

The 90 km and 65 km are races

Ride your own race. PPK regroups at the finish meeting point, not on the road.

Questions

Is this a PPK event?
No. PPK travels to the Heritage Ride Taiping together; the organiser runs the race.
Can I still enter?
Entries closed on 17 September 2026. Riders without an entry are not allowed on the routes.
Can family come?
Yes, in the cars and at the meal. Arrange their seats with your driver.
Who do I ask?
Ask in the PPK WhatsApp group.

How to join

Car seats, the meal and meetups are listings on Group rides, titled "Taiping trip · …". Sign up there with your PPK account.
