Before you go

The organiser's rules

  • Your rider bib and bike flag number are compulsory at check-in.
  • On the 90 and 65 km, fix the bike bib with the timing chip to the front of the bike, 1 cm clear of any carbon.
  • Wear a helmet and gloves.
  • The 90 and 65 km routes split at the 4.8 km junction: follow the route signs and the marshals.

Checked 17 Oct 2026 against facebook.com (1, 2).

Bring

  • Helmet, gloves, front and rear lights (it's dark at the start)
  • IC, confirmation slip, bib
  • Spare tube, pump and tools
  • Water and food for your distance
  • A rain jacket
  • Breakfast for 4:30 am
  • PPK kit for the group photo

Questions

Is this a PPK event?
No. PPK travels to the Heritage Ride Taiping together; the organiser runs the race.
Can I still enter?
Entries closed on 17 September 2026. Riders without an entry are not allowed on the routes.
Can family come?
Yes, in the cars and at meals. Arrange their seats with your driver.
Who do I ask?
The PPK WhatsApp group.

Run by GoSportz, with Kelab Berbasikal Elit and the office of the Member of Parliament for Taiping. PPK does not run this event.
