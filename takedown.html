Deleting your data and taking pages down

PPK is run by volunteers. This page says how to ask us to delete your account or take a page down, how long it takes, and what we can't remove.

Delete your PPK account

  • How to ask: message PPK through linktr.ee/rideppk, or ask a ride organiser. Tell us the email address you sign in with. We email that address to check the request is yours.
  • What goes: your sign-in, your profile, your safety details (your emergency contact), your ride sign-ups, your roles and your achievements. Rides you organised stay, without your name.
  • How long: within 21 days of your reply to our email. We tell you when it's done.
  • Backups: our encrypted backups keep a copy until they are deleted. Each time we make a new backup, we delete the ones more than 30 days old.

Take a page down

  • How to ask: the same way. Send us the page's link and tell us what you want removed.
  • What we do: we remove your athlete page and your name from the results pages. Your times may stay, without your name, so other riders' places stay right.
  • How long: within 21 days.

What stays

  • The site is published from a public repository on GitHub, and its history keeps earlier versions of every page. Removing a page from that history is possible, but it can't be undone, so PPK's site admin decides each case.
  • Search engines and web archives may keep copies for a while. We can ask Google to drop a removed page from its results.

Who decides

PPK's site admin decides each request and replies within the times above. If we can't do what you ask, we say why.
